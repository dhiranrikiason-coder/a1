# Design Registry for windbreakgear.com

## Aesthetic Vision: Alpine Storm Performance & High-Tech Aerodynamic Minimalism
- **Color Palette**: Glacier Cobalt (`#1B4D7E`), Deep Alpine Slate (`#16212B`), Storm Cyan (`#00A896`), High-Vis Safety Orange (`#FF6B35`), Polar Ice Fog (`#F3F7FA`), Dark Charcoal (`#10171E`).
- **Typography**: Display Headings (`Syne`, sans-serif / `Cabinet Grotesk`) paired with razor-sharp Technical Sans (`Plus Jakarta Sans`, sans-serif).
- **Header Fix Mandate**: Desktop nav presents 5 primary destinations (`Home`, `About`, `Jackets`, `Blog`, `Contact`) + CTA button. Policy links are classified with `.nav-item-policy` and hidden on desktop (`>1140px`), seamlessly accessible in the mobile drawer (`<=1140px`) and footer.
- **Policy Pages Mandate**: All 4 legal/policy pages contain comprehensive paragraphs with 5 to 6 lines each, plus the dedicated **Customer Care, Outerwear Inquiries & Legal Compliance Card**.
- **Performance**: Zero bulky dependencies, vanilla modern JavaScript, 100% responsive off-canvas drawer with backdrop blur.
