Our Bedrock

Engineered for High-Velocity Weather Extremes

WindBreakGear was conceived in the high alpine cirques of the Pacific Northwest, where sudden meteorological shifts transform temperate afternoon ascents into freezing wind storms.

From Glacial Ridges to Mercer Street

Traditional windbreakers forced outdoor athletes into an unacceptable compromise: either choose a windproof plastic bag that caused profuse sweating, or choose a breathable garment that allowed freezing summit winds to cut straight to the core.

At our Mercer Street atelier in New York, we engineered a third path. By pairing micro-porous membrane laminates with diamond-grid Cordura ripstop and ultrasonic seam bonding, we created technical windbreakers that are impervious to gale-force winds while venting metabolic heat with unyielding efficiency.

The Three Cornerstones

Our Non-Negotiable Standards

1. Absolute Wind Defense

Every production lot is tested in high-velocity airflow chambers to guarantee 0.00 CFM air permeability across all primary chest and shoulder panels.

2. Eco-Friendly Hydrophobics

We reject toxic fluorocarbons. All WindBreakGear shells utilize 100% plant-based C0 DWR coatings that protect against precipitation without polluting watersheds.

3. Field Repairability

Built for lifelong alpine service. We engineer our garments with modular pattern geometry that facilitates straightforward needle-and-tape repairs in remote basecamps.
