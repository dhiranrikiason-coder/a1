Textile Physics & Aerodynamics

The Science of Boundary Layer Wind Deflection

When wind strikes conventional outdoor clothing, high dynamic pressure forces cold air through woven fabric pores, displacing the microscopic layer of warm air trapped adjacent to the skin.

Our proprietary StormWeave textile structure integrates billions of microscopic pores per square centimeter—each 20,000 times smaller than a water droplet, yet 700 times larger than a water vapor molecule. This tortuous pore matrix stops 60 mph gale-force winds dead in their tracks while permitting metabolic perspiration vapor to diffuse freely outwards.

Technical Guidance

Frequently Asked Outerwear Inquiries

Expert advice on DWR re-activation, washing technical membranes, and selecting the optimal jacket weight.

What is the difference between a windbreaker and a full rain jacket?
A traditional windbreaker focuses primarily on zero CFM wind permeability and maximum breathability for high-aerobic activity. Our hybrid wind jackets incorporate 3-layer waterproof membranes and fully taped seams, delivering the ultimate combination: total wind blockage alongside 28,000mm hydrostatic rain protection.
How do I wash and re-activate the C0 DWR water-repellent coating?
Machine wash your technical shell in warm water (104°F / 40°C) with a liquid technical outerwear wash. Never use powder detergents, fabric softeners, or bleach. Tumble dry on low heat for twenty minutes to thermally re-activate the fluorocarbon-free hydrophobic DWR surface molecules.
How do I choose between an ultralight shell and an alpine hardshell?
For running, cycling, and fast-and-light summer ascents, the 115g Zephyr Packable Shell offers unmatched breathability and compact stowage. For winter mountaineering, ice climbing, and multi-day trekking in severe weather, the 3-layer Summit Ridge shell provides heavy-duty Cordura abrasion resistance and full helmet coverage.
Do you accommodate custom sleeve lengths and athletic layering fits?
Yes. Our Mercer Street atelier provides bespoke sizing consultations for climbers with extended reach or individuals needing tailored layering allowances for expedition down mid-layers. Visit our Contact page to arrange a consultation.
