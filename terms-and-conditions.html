3. Three-Year Alpine Shell Warranty and Repair Covenant

Every technical jacket crafted by WindBreakGear is backed by our comprehensive Three-Year Alpine Shell Limited Warranty, protecting the original registered purchaser against structural defects in materials, seam tape delamination, and zipper mechanical failures. If a jacket experiences premature breakdown of its 0 CFM wind barrier or membrane failure under ordinary non-commercial alpine use, WindBreakGear will inspect, repair, or replace the garment at our sole discretion without labor charges. This warranty specifically reflects our confidence in the diamond-grid ripstop nylon and ultrasonic seam sealing methodologies developed within our Mercer Street engineering laboratories. To file a formal warranty assessment claim, patrons must present proof of purchase alongside detailed photographic documentation illustrating the localized fabric anomaly.

This limited warranty specifically excludes damages arising from external traumatic punctures, crampon tears, open campfire burns, abrasive rockfall lacerations, improper industrial tumble-drying, or unauthorized home modifications. Because high-performance technical shells are designed for rugged wilderness exploration, external mechanical abrasion is a recognized reality of alpine mountaineering rather than a fabric defect. For non-warrantied trail tears and bushwhack snags, our Mercer Street atelier maintains an affordable technical repair program, utilizing factory-matched Gore-Tex patches and pneumatic pressure presses to restore windproof integrity. We believe in maximizing garment service life and supporting circular sustainability, keeping functional shells performing on the summits for generations rather than consigning damaged apparel to municipal landfills.

4. Returns, Exchanges, and Fitting Restitution Guidelines

We recognize that achieving the flawless ergonomic fit is imperative when selecting technical alpine outerwear designed to accommodate insulating mid-layers and climbing harnesses. WindBreakGear affords patrons an unconditional thirty-day return and exchange window from the date of physical courier delivery, provided that the garment remains in brand-new, unworn condition with all factory tags, Cohaesive hood adjusters, and technical booklets intact. Patrons are encouraged to evaluate the fit and arm articulation indoors over their typical base layers prior to field deployment in outdoor wilderness environments. Garments that exhibit outdoor trail odors, campfire soot, mud staining, or skin oil residue upon inspection will be rejected and returned to the sender at their expense.

To initiate a seamless return or size exchange, patrons must generate an authorized Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA) tracking number via our Mercer Street customer care portal prior to dispatching parcels. Return courier shipping expenses within the continental United States are fully subsidized by WindBreakGear for initial size exchanges, ensuring that our customers achieve their optimum ergonomic silhouette without friction. For full refunds, return postage is deducted from the credit disbursement, which is processed to the original payment token within five to seven business days following laboratory inspection at our facility. Custom bespoke orders tailored with monogrammed embroidery or non-standard sleeve extensions are recognized as final sales and remain ineligible for standard commercial return.
