3. Technical Membrane Maintenance & DWR Wash Protocol

WindbreakGear jackets are engineered with advanced 3-layer ePTFE microporous membranes and eco-friendly C0 durable water repellent (DWR) chemical finishes, granting them exceptional breathability and wind blocking. However, customers are contractually required to adhere strictly to our maintenance protocols, which mandate machine washing in warm water (104°F / 40°C) with a liquid technical detergent formulated specifically for waterproof membranes. Severe chemical damage resulting from the use of powdered detergents, fabric softeners, biological enzymes, or chlorine bleach strips the protective hydrophobic finish, clogging micropores and permanently voiding all breathability and leakage warranties. Customers must always wash technical shells inside-out with all zippers and pocket flaps securely closed.

Our technical shells demand periodic heat activation to maintain their hydrophobic water-beading performance across extended mountain seasons. Following washing, tumbling the garment on low heat for twenty minutes re-aligns the fluorocarbon-free hydrocarbon polymer chains, restoring full water-shedding performance without damaging the waterproof membrane. Failure to maintain garments according to these instructions accelerates surface wetting (wet-out), which severely compromises breathability and introduces internal condensation that can be mistaken for seam leakage. Detailed maintenance manuals are included inside every packaging carton and remain accessible through our digital technical journal at all times. Customers assume full operational responsibility for following these care instructions, ensuring their outerwear remains fully protective across years of rugged alpine service.
